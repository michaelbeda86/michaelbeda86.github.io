03 — About

Who I am

I am a software developer based in Dar es Salaam, interested in how good software quietly solves everyday problems.

I graduated from the University of Dodoma with a Bachelor of Science in Instructional Design and Information Technology, and I have been building for the web ever since.

Most of my work sits where web and mobile meet: a clean front end, a sensible back end, and an interface people can use without being taught. I am always learning something new and taking on work that stretches me.
